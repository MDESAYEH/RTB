# VISUAL SYSTEM — ROAD TO BAL / TRIPOLI 2027

## 1. Brand idea
THE COURT BECOMES THE ROAD: continuous compositions connect club identity, history and tournament action. Internal art direction only. Public identity remains ROAD TO BAL, TRIPOLI 2027 and THE ROAD. No metric cards or repeated bordered panels.

## 2. Road line rules
Tokens: thickness 2px; active thickness 3px; normal #BEA574 at 38% opacity; active #F06428 at 55–100%; muted gold at 20%. Curve radii: 40px mobile, 80px desktop. One continuous primary path per composition, with rounded corners and round caps. Enter and exit at a viewport edge; carry the same tangent into the next scene. Mobile follows a vertical spine with short lateral detours. Intersections are deliberate anchors beside numbers or logos, never across body copy. Endpoints use a 6px square or a single open arrow; arrows use the same stroke and 45-degree direction. Court sidelines can share the path; secondary geometry stays muted. Match/timeline separators extend from the spine, rather than forming enclosing boxes. Page paths may adapt to content, but must retain these tokens and grammar; do not invent new stroke styles.

## 3. Court geometry rules
Allowed vocabulary: center circle, half-circle arc, sideline, baseline and free-throw geometry. Maximum two geometry families per scene. Center circle identifies a host or pivotal stage; arcs support transitions; sidelines organize encounters; free-throw geometry reserves photography space. No complete technical court diagram unless it communicates team positioning. Geometry is non-interactive and hidden from assistive technology.

## 4. 21—25 rules
Only three forms: monumental outline (1px muted gold, one opening appearance); solid event-date display (orange at the match transition); compact metadata signature (readable sans-serif with October 2026). Never imply 2027 is the calendar date of the Tripoli event. No additional variants, textures or repeating background patterns.

## 5. Typography hierarchy
Latin condensed: club names, scores, major numbers and campaign statements only. Arabic: major editorial statements and narrative. Supporting labels use calm sans-serif. Minimum desktop/mobile sizes: micro labels 12px/12px, supporting information 14px/14px, Arabic body 16px/16px. Arabic body line-height 1.9–2; headings 1.35–1.5. Latin labels line-height 1.4. Reading columns 45–65 characters; short mobile paragraphs use natural RTL alignment. Oversized type may crop only when the word remains legible; content and controls never crop.

## 6. Color roles
Ink #080E14: campaign ground and media depth. Paper #EEECE5: editorial reading ground. Orange #F06428: active path, decisive number, directional interruption. Gold #BEA574: supporting geometry and tournament context. Small paper-ground labels use darker gold #805329 for contrast. Transition surfaces with arcs or continuous edges, not alternating rectangular sections. Preserve logo colors.

## 7. Photography rules
Only identity-verified assets with approved usage. Full-bleed basketball action is allowed in signature openings and editorial features. Athlete cutouts require sufficient source resolution and natural edges; never substitute a lone cutout for a meaningful group-action scene. Type behind athletes and number masks must preserve faces, ball and factual reading. Separate desktop/mobile editorial crops. History copy, group positions and utility tables do not need photography. Typography and geometry work without an image; no stock or unverified placeholder image.

## 8. Motion rules
One line draw/travel at a time; clipped campaign reveal up to 800ms; subtle logo reveal may share that sequence. Number/score motion only accompanies a real update. No scroll-jacking or extensive parallax. Reduced motion stops path travel and reveals final content immediately. Geometry never blocks input or triggers layout shifts.

## 9. Expressiveness levels A/B/C
A — Signature/cinematic: Homepage, THE ROAD, Team Hero, Live Game Center. Monumental type, one strong path and optional photography.
B — Editorial: Teams, club stories, News. Narrow readable copy, selective oversized achievements, restrained geometry.
C — Information-first: Matches, Standings, Stats. Compact path markers and separators, readable dense information. Admin remains functional: accessible typography and state accents only, no poster compositions.

## 10. Mobile adaptation rules
Re-compose at 390px; validate 360–1920px. Vertical path, independently positioned club name/logo and achievement. Group positions form a staggered vertical journey with the host centered and largest. Normalize logo canvases with contain and per-asset padding, without recoloring or cropping. Touch targets at least 44px, visible keyboard focus. Labels remain 12px minimum and body 16px. Avoid path crossings through paragraphs, clipped Arabic lines or a fixed navigation covering content. Page height follows content, not desktop proportions.
